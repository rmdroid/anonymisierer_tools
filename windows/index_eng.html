Three layers.
One result.

Rules catch structured details. Built-in NER adds people, places and organizations. A local AI check is only added if you switch it on.

Regex.

Email addresses, phone numbers, dates, IBANs and other identifiers. Define your own terms or deliberately exclude them.

NER, built in.

Named entity recognition finds people, places and organizations. It ships with the app and needs neither internet access nor extra software.

Local AI, optional.

Set up a small model directly in the app or connect an existing Ollama or LM Studio installation. Regex and NER work without it.

The app interface is currently available in German only.

Local AI is an experimental add-on.

It can extend detection but does not guarantee complete detection. Setup downloads such as the small model require an internet connection. Anonymization itself runs on your PC.

FAQ

Good to know.

Is my text uploaded?

No. Anonymization runs on your PC, without an anonymization server. Whatever you then paste into other services is processed under their terms.

Do I need Ollama or LM Studio?

No. Regex and NER are built in. For the optional AI check, you can set up a small model in the app or connect an existing local Ollama or LM Studio installation.

How does restoration work?

When enabled, the app keeps the mapping between placeholders and original details from the last session in memory, for five minutes at most. This lets you restore the real references locally in an edited result.

Can the Windows app edit images and screenshots?

No. The current Windows version processes text. The image and screenshot features of the Apple apps are not automatically part of the Windows app.

Is the app available in English?

Not yet. The app interface is currently German only.

Which PCs does it run on?

The app is available on the Microsoft Store with builds for x64 and ARM64.

Is the Mac app included?

No. The Windows app, the Mac app and the iPhone/iPad app are separate purchases.
