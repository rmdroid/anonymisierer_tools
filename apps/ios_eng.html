Frequently asked questions

Does anonymization run locally?

Yes. Text, images and files are not uploaded to an external cloud for anonymization.

Does the app work without Apple Intelligence?

Yes. It uses rule-based detection, Natural Language and local system features. Apple Intelligence adds to it when available.

Can I anonymize screenshots manually?

Yes. In addition to automatic detection, you mark your own areas and blur or black them out.

Is the Mac app included?

No. The iPhone/iPad app and the Mac app are separate apps.
