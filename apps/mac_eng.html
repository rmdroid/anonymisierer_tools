Four moves. No change to your process.

  1. ⌘C

    Copy text

    Select the sensitive passage and copy it as usual, from mail, documents, the browser or notes.

  2. ⌘⇧A

    Press the shortcut

    The clipboard content is anonymized locally on your Mac. No cloud, no account.

  3. ⌘V

    Paste and review

    Paste the anonymized version. For longer texts, the editor stays the calm review view.

  4. ⌘⇧D

    Restore real names

    After the AI reply, a second shortcut puts the real names back locally. The mapping lives in memory only and is deleted after five minutes at most.

What the Mac app detects and does

People

Names and typical name contexts, with stable placeholders for readable results.

Addresses

Streets, house numbers, postcodes and places as one connected structure.

Contact details

Email, phone and fax in typical German formats.

Identifiers

IBAN, case numbers, social security numbers and similar patterns.

Images and screenshots

Detect OCR text, faces and barcodes, then blur or black out missing areas by hand.

Three local layers

Regex, Apple NaturalLanguage and optional Apple Intelligence, plus your own rules and exclusions.

The real app

Support, not the final word

Rule-based detection can be incomplete in individual cases. Apple language recognition and optional Apple Intelligence only add to it. Always review before sharing or publishing.

Frequently asked questions

Does anonymization run locally?

Yes. Detection and replacement run on your Mac, without cloud upload and without an account.

Do I need Apple Intelligence?

No. The app works fully without it. If Apple Intelligence is available, it adds an extra layer for people and places.

How does restoration work?

When enabled, the app keeps the mapping of the last session in memory only. A second shortcut replaces the placeholders in the AI reply with the real details. The mapping is deleted after five minutes at most.

Are team licenses available?

Yes. Outside the App Store, the app is available directly, including team licenses. Contact us via the form.

Are the iPhone or Windows apps included?

No. Mac, iPhone/iPad and Windows are separate apps with separate purchases.
